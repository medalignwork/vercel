What is MedAlign?

A matching layer between patients and clinics.

MedAlign is an online patient-to-clinic matching platform, free for patients. Instead of sorting through search results, outdated directories and paid ads, you answer a few guided questions and see specialized clinics ranked by how well they fit.

The matching process considers goals, symptoms, treatment interests, medical considerations, preferred location, lifestyle and desired therapies. The aim is to help you understand your options before choosing whether to connect with a clinic.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Understand the assessment, matching process and what happens when you choose a clinic.

What is MedAlign (Med Align)?+

MedAlign is a patient-to-clinic matching platform that is free for patients. It uses a short health assessment to show personalized recommendations for specialized wellness and medical clinics, ranked by fit.

Is MedAlign free for patients?+

Yes. The assessment and clinic matches are free, and no credit card is required. Clinics pay per qualified consultation.

How long does the assessment take?+

The assessment has eight short steps and takes about four minutes. Scheduling a consultation afterwards takes about a minute.

Do I need to create an account?+

No account is needed to take the assessment. MedAlign asks for your location and contact details at the very end, after you have seen your matches.

How are clinics ranked?+

MedAlign states that matches are ranked by fit, never by ad spend. Rankings are based on how closely a clinic's services line up with your answers.

Is MedAlign a healthcare provider?+

No. MedAlign is a matching platform. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. Matches are informational and are not medical advice, a diagnosis or a clinical referral.

Does MedAlign guarantee patients to clinics?+

No. MedAlign aims to send better-matched scheduled consultations, but it does not guarantee patient volume. Clinics are billed per qualified consultation, with no long-term contracts.

Is this the same as medalign.com or AlignMed?+

No. The MedAlign described here is the patient-to-clinic matching platform at getmedalign.com. It is unrelated to the other organizations that share a similar name.
